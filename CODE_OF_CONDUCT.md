# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We aim to provide a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone participating in Arrakis Control Dashboard, regardless of background, identity, or experience.

## Expected behavior

- Treat others with respect and patience.
- Give constructive feedback and assume good faith where reasonable.
- Respect differing viewpoints and technical experience.
- Protect other people's privacy and personal information.
- Accept reasonable moderation and maintainer decisions.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or discriminatory insults.
- Sexual harassment or unwanted sexual attention.
- Personal attacks, sustained disruption, or targeted bullying.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This code applies to project repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, discussions, and official project community spaces, as well as public representation of the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to project maintainers through a non-public contact method published in the repository or its official community channels. Do not disclose sensitive reports in public issues. Maintainers should handle reports discreetly, limit access to information, assess conflicts of interest, and take proportionate action.

Possible actions include a warning, content removal, temporary participation restrictions, or a permanent ban. Decisions may consider severity, repeated behavior, and community safety.

If a maintainer is involved in a report, seek another uninvolved maintainer or moderator. Do not promise absolute confidentiality where safety or legal obligations require disclosure.

## Attribution
